Methodology

What this demo is proving

  1. Start with a normal BOLT11 invoice. The invoice should usually be a Bitcoin mainnet invoice beginning with lnbc.
  2. URL-encode the invoice. The exact invoice string is appended to https://cash.app/launch/lightning/.
  3. Open from a direct user tap. On a phone with Cash App installed, the universal link can open Cash App with the payment loaded.
  4. Keep a manual fallback. If the universal link fails, copy the BOLT11 invoice and paste it into Cash App's Lightning flow.
  5. Optional sample invoice. The demo can request a 1 sat invoice from austin@vlt.ge by resolving its Lightning Address and calling the LNURL-pay callback with amount=1000 millisats.
